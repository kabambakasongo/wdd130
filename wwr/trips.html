Three ways to run the river, from a gentle family float to a two day expedition through the rapids.

Trip Details

Salmon River Run

Half day on calm water with a few easy riffles. Perfect for families and first time paddlers. Lunch and gear included.

Los Dientes del Diablo

Full day of class III and IV rapids with a riverside barbecue. Recommended for paddlers in good shape with some experience.

Whitewater Express

Two days on the water with one night camped on the bank. Class IV rapids, all meals provided, small groups only.
